instead of sending my resume into the void, i ran my job search like an outbound motion: find the companies that need GTM help right now, give them a real reason to care, and reach out on LinkedIn.

  1. build the campaignconnected Trigify + HeyReach to Claude Code and told it my ICP: who i'm targeting, where, and what size.
  2. find the signalsocial listening in Trigify for companies talking about needing GTM help right now: hiring GTM engineers, posting about new clients.
  3. keep the offer strongmedium effort so it personalises at scale, low stakes so replying feels easy. copy stays human.
  4. send on linkedin, not emailwarm connections, no deliverability headaches. warmed the account from 10 to 40 a day in HeyReach.
  5. automate the loopClaude built a workflow that qualifies leads, pushes them into HeyReach and drops updates in Slack.
